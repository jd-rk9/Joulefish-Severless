Subject: Commission participation in the First International Conference for the Just
Transition Away from Fossil Fuels
The First International Conference for the Just Transition Away from Fossil Fuels will be cohosted by Colombia and the Netherlands in Santa Marta, Colombia, on 28 -29 April 2026.
The conference was announced by the Colombian Minister of Environment, Irene Vélez
Torres, and the Dutch Deputy Prime Minister and Minister for Climate Policy, Sophie
Hermans, at COP30. It represents a critical opportunity to advance international cooperation
on accelerating the phasing out of fossil fuels in line with climate science and justice
imperatives and in particular the Bélem Action Mechanism for a Just Transition, approved by
Parties at COP30 as part of the Bélem Package.
Given the EU’s stated commitment to global climate leadership and the objectives of the
European Green Deal:
1. How does the Commission view the Santa Marta conference’s role as an international
forum for advancing a global, equitable and time-bound phase-out of fossil fuels, in line
with the Paris Agreement and the EU’s climate objectives?
2. Will the Commission be officially represented at the conference and if so at what
political level?
3. Has the Commission adopted a position in relation to the conference, does it intend to
present concrete proposals, including timelines and commitments related to the phaseout of fossil fuels, both within the EU and at the international level, and will it publish
these proposals?
